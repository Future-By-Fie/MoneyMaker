# MoneyMaker™ — Trust, Rights & Platform Considerations

## Financial accuracy
A bank balance is not the same as discretionary spending power. Reserve unpaid obligations and a safety buffer. Explain what is included and what is not. A stale estimate must not be portrayed as current.

## Reconciliation
Suggested balances are predictions, not bank data. Ask users to compare with their actual balance before confirming. Handle transfers, incoming payments, card holds and paid bills without double-counting.

## Privacy
Minimize financial data collection. Avoid public balance sharing. Implement consent, deletion, encryption, retention limits and GDPR review before handling production data. A demo should use synthetic data only.

## Banking
No bank connection is needed for the MVP. Optional future bank connectivity must use an appropriately authorized provider and clear user consent. Never ask users for online banking passwords.

## Music and ringtones
- Spotify is an **illustrative potential ecosystem participant**, not a partner.
- Streaming playback is different from ringtone licensing.
- Licensed commercial tracks require appropriate recording and composition rights.
- iOS and Android impose different ringtone customization restrictions.
- Use original or properly licensed sounds in prototypes.
- Avoid implying automatic system-ringtone switching is available across platforms.

## Brand
MoneyMaker™ is a **working name**. Conduct trademark, app-store and domain clearance before investing heavily in public launch assets.

## Scope discipline
The finance utility must work independently of Flex. No complex social feed, transaction categorization, gamified pressure or unlicensed music in the MVP.

## Disclaimer
This repository is a concept and prototype planning resource. It does not provide regulated financial advice or live financial services.
