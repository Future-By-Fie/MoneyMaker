# MoneyMaker™ — Product Vision

**When your money talks, everyone listens.**

## 0. Executive Summary

MoneyMaker is a mobile-first personal finance concept for young adults, initially focused on ages **18–28** (secondary audience 29–40).

It answers one question: **How much can I comfortably spend today?**

A simple calculation considers a user-confirmed balance, known future bills, safety buffer, next payday and reserved goals. Users can shift their spending freedom across dates using a visual slider, such as reserving 700 kr per day for the final week before payday.

The standard experience uses periodic **balance reconciliation**, not manual transaction logging. A regulated bank-data connection may be offered later as an option.

## Product philosophy

> Checking your money should feel like checking the weather. Not filing your taxes.

- **3 seconds:** Understand today's estimate.
- **3 taps:** Set a goal, where practical.
- **Zero expense categorization:** We do not need to know what you bought.
- **Trust before excitement:** Stale balance data is visible.
- **Personality without pressure:** Celebrate progress, never shame users or encourage unnecessary spending.

## Core

1. **Today:** One prominent estimated spending allowance.
2. **My Goal:** Reserve an amount for a specific date or spending period. Recalculate the remaining daily allowance instantly.
3. **Money Check:** Show a clearly labeled *estimated* balance, let the user compare it with their bank and confirm or edit it.
4. **Morning Money:** Optional daily summary only when the estimate is sufficiently fresh.

## Flex — optional entertainment layer

MoneyMaker Flex adds an expressive, playful brand identity. Users may select original sound profiles and celebrate reaching personal goals.

Longer-term concepts include regional MoneyMaker Charts, monthly voting and licensed ringtone experiences. Spotify may be cited as an example of an established music platform, **not** an existing partner.

Commercial music streaming does not grant ringtone rights. Actual ringtone functionality is subject to music licensing and mobile OS capabilities.

## Competitive hypothesis

Daily allowance calculators and budgeting apps already exist. The differentiating hypothesis is **radically simple future allocation + low-friction balance checks + playful music-driven identity**.

This is a hypothesis, not a proven market advantage.

## Success criteria

We should test whether users:
- Understand the primary number without explanation.
- Can create a future goal intuitively.
- Reconcile their balance willingly without a bank connection.
- Trust the freshness status.
- Return after seven days without excessive reminders.
