## ☀️ The weather forecast for your wallet

**420 kr to spend today.**

That's the idea. No expense-category maze. No spreadsheets. No mandatory bank connection.

MoneyMaker helps you understand what you can comfortably spend **today** while making room for the things you want **tomorrow**.

> **Checking your money should feel like checking the weather. Not filing your taxes.**

## 🔍 Designed for trust

A daily allowance is an **estimate**, not a bank guarantee.

MoneyMaker shows when the underlying balance was last confirmed. Older data visibly becomes less trustworthy; the app asks for a quick **Money Check** instead of pretending it knows your current balance.

A suggested balance is **not** a live bank reading. Users must verify it before confirmation. Future optional Open Banking integration would require an authorized provider.

## 🚀 What we're building first

**MVP:**
- Today's estimated spending allowance
- Payday, obligations and safety buffer
- One simple future goal or reserved spending period
- Fast balance reconciliation
- Balance freshness indicator
- Optional Morning Money reminder, where supported

**Not in MVP:** automatic bank sync, commercial music, ringtone automation, community voting, investment advice or payment processing.
